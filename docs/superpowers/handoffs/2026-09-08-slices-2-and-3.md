## Slice 2 — Nouri plans the day

The planner exists. `planDay` takes your shift, the day's prayer times and a
task list, and returns the four blocks with real times. Home shows it, and the
«معاينة» label is gone because it is no longer a preview.

Everything from the worked example is in it: sleep sized first and never cut,
heavy tasks only where you are home and free, light ones riding the commute
and the work break, breaks that scale with the effort just finished, knowledge
time as one rotating block, and prayers as anchors that everything else fits
around.

**The six open questions are answered in `docs/planner-decisions.md`**, each
the safest reversible way, each a setting or a constant away from changing.
The short version: what does not fit is deferred and *always named*; a night
day has its own shape; a day off is left loose; re-planning is deterministic
from now; user tasks stay in the calendar for the moment; meals get windows
rather than times.

**Three rules the tests forced that the worked example only implied.** Fajr
outranks the shift's wake time — bounding the day at 05:00 filed a 04:07 fajr
as "before the day began" and dropped it. The hour before leaving for work is
light-only, because that hour is fajr, athkar, breakfast and the door. And the
evening cannot open at maghrib on a shift that ends after it.

**One real bug, found because a day off looked fuller than a work day.** The
first window model advanced a window's start past anything placed inside it,
so a prayer at 11:46 in a window running 04:07–18:04 moved the start to 12:01
and silently threw away seven usable hours. Windows split now.

## Slice 3 — water, and the fast that governs it

A water card in البدن: a count against a target of eight, a plus and a minus,
and a nudge after each prayer. Reminders ride the prayers because five times a
day you already stop what you are doing — "after every prayer" is a rule
someone keeps, and a two-hourly alarm is not.

**The part that took the care is the fast.** Water is fine during 16/8 — that
window restricts food, not drink — but during a religious fast nothing passes
the lips between fajr and maghrib. A nudge to drink at noon on a Monday you
are fasting would be Nouri telling you to break it. So on a day marked as a
fast the daytime reminders are simply absent, and the evening one says «فطرت».

**The marker is your word, never inferred.** Nouri suggests the sunnah fasts
but has no way of knowing whether one was kept.

## A review pass, and what it found

With the features done I went back over the night's work looking for the bug
class that had already produced three — state read after an `await`. It found
more than that, and none of it was speculative: every one is either
demonstrated by a test that fails without the fix, or measured on this machine.

**The plan changed depending on when you opened it.** Two faults, one instinct.
Clamping windows to `now` made a flexible task land at "now" and keep moving —
15:45, then 15:55 ten minutes later — chasing the clock and never arriving.
Fixing that exposed the worse one: past prayers were filtered out *before*
placement, so a fajr that had already happened stopped consuming its fifteen
minutes and everything shifted. The same day planned at noon came out
different from the same day planned at dawn. `now` is gone from the planner
entirely; this reverses one of the six answers and
`docs/planner-decisions.md` says so.

**Four dates were stepped by offset rather than constructed** — the bug class
this project documented after it moved the adhan by an hour. Measured here:

```
2026-10-29 00:00  +24h -> 2026-10-29 23:00   (the same date)
                 built -> 2026-10-30 00:00
```

The fourteen-day alarm window therefore covered **thirteen** days once a year,
and the weekly report showed six days and a duplicate. I checked before
claiming it: the first probe suggested no repeats, because it stepped
incrementally while the real loop steps `today + i`. Then reverted the fix and
watched the test fail with "Expected 14, Actual 13".

**Editing a reminder into the past left its old alarm firing.** `arm` only ever
added; it establishes the truth now.

**`logPrayer` read the notification service after its write**, so an unmount
mid-write would skip the follow-up cancellation — precisely the bug `0690711`
had to fix, reached through a different door.

**Counters read backwards in RTL.** «٠ / ٣» says three of zero. I had flagged
this as your call; it turned out the app already used «من» in five places and
a slash in three, so the three were the odd ones out.

Four guard tests now fail the build on these shapes, and they are listed in
STATUS.

## A note on the machine

**Windows killed the emulator twice for low memory**, both times during a
Gradle build running alongside it — the second time on the final release
build. Nothing was lost on either occasion: the work was committed, and the
APK finished.

This machine has 15.9 GB and had about 5.5 GB free when it happened. Building
and emulating at the same time does not fit. The pattern that works is to
**build first, then start the emulator, then install** — never a build while
the emulator is up. Starting it with `-memory 1536` helps but does not make
the concurrent case safe.

Worth knowing before a long device-testing session: expect to stop the
emulator before each rebuild.
